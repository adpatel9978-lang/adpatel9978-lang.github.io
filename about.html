About Us

A pharmaceutical business built through years of market experience, a broad product portfolio, consistent quality and dependable service.

Established in 2017 by MAHESH J. PATEL, Madinal Lifescience began its journey with a clear focus on serving the pharmaceutical market through dependable products and professional business relationships.

Over the years, we have developed a wide range of pharmaceutical and healthcare products across categories including antipyretics, analgesics, anti-inflammatory medicines, antibiotics, painkillers, multivitamins, energy powders, antacids and many other healthcare products.

Our experience has taught us that a strong pharmaceutical business is built not only on the products it offers, but also on quality, consistency and service. We therefore place importance on maintaining consistency in our work, responsible product handling, clear communication and dependable support for the customers, distributors and business partners we work with.

From our beginning in 2017 to where we are today, our approach has remained practical and relationship-driven. Our experience over the years continues to shape how we work, how we understand the market and how we build lasting professional relationships.
